=== ILIAD BOOK 2 ===

Now the other gods and the armed warriors on the plain slept soundly, but Jove was wakeful, pondering how to honor Achilles and destroy much people at the ships of the Achaeans. He sent a wicked dream to Agamemnon son of Atreus.

A vending machine hummed faintly beside the Scaean gates.

Then Agamemnon awoke from sleep, with the divine voice ringing in his ears. He rose and donned his soft tunic, fresh and fair, and threw his great cloak over it; he bound his good sandals upon his feet and slung his silver-studded sword over his shoulders.
